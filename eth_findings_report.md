# ETH Violations: Computational Methods Survey

## 1. Exact Diagonalization (ED) Studies of ETH

### 1.1 Quantum Master Equation from ETH
- **Title:** Quantum master equation from the eigenstate thermalization hypothesis
- **Authors:** Peter O'Donovan, Philipp Strasberg, Kavan Modi, John Goold, Mark T. Mitchison
- **Year:** 2025
- **Description:** Derives a quantum master equation for systems weakly coupled to chaotic finite-sized baths, using ETH to show Markovianity emerges from the ETH spectral function.
- **Identifier:** arXiv:2411.07706, Phys. Rev. B 112, 014312 (2025)

### 1.2 Fragmented Eigenstate Thermalization in Long-Range Models
- **Title:** Fragmented eigenstate thermalization versus robust integrability in long-range models
- **Authors:** Soumya Kanti Pal, Lea F. Santos
- **Year:** 2025
- **Description:** Shows that in fully connected models, chaos emerges within symmetry sectors, leading to fragmented ETH realization, clarifying how microcanonical shells can be constructed.
- **Identifier:** arXiv:2508.00077

### 1.3 Scaling Theory of Fading Ergodicity
- **Title:** Scaling theory of fading ergodicity
- **Authors:** Rafał Świętek, Miroslav Hopjan, Carlo Vanoni, Antonello Scardicchio, Lev Vidmar
- **Year:** 2025
- **Description:** Introduces a scaling theory for the precursor regime of many-body ergodicity breaking, showing two-parameter scaling governs the entire ergodic regime.
- **Identifier:** arXiv:2412.15331, Phys. Rev. Lett. 135, 170401 (2025)

### 1.4 ETH and Quantum Error Correction
- **Title:** Approximate quantum error correction, eigenstate thermalization and the chaos bound
- **Authors:** Shozab Qasim, Jason Pollack
- **Year:** 2025
- **Description:** Establishes a precise link between quantum error correction, thermalization, and quantum chaos in systems satisfying ETH, showing the chaos bound constrains code error.
- **Identifier:** arXiv:2510.26758

### 1.5 Hydrodynamics and ETH
- **Title:** Hydrodynamics and the eigenstate thermalization hypothesis
- **Authors:** J. Wang, X. Xu, L. Mazza, D. Poletti
- **Year:** 2025
- **Description:** Studies the relationship between hydrodynamic behavior and ETH, showing how ETH correlations emerge from nonlinear hydrodynamics.
- **Identifier:** Phys. Rev. X 15, 011059 (2025)

### 1.6 ETH Correlations via Nonlinear Hydrodynamics
- **Title:** Eigenstate thermalization hypothesis correlations via nonlinear hydrodynamics
- **Authors:** J. Wang, R. Mishra, T.-H. Yang, L. V. Pappalardi
- **Year:** 2026
- **Description:** Extends ETH analysis to correlation functions using nonlinear hydrodynamic frameworks.
- **Identifier:** Phys. Rev. Lett. 136, 130402 (2026)

## 4. Cold Atom Experiments Probing Thermalization

- **Authors:** Ceren B. Dağ, Simeon I. Mistakidis, Amos Chan, H. R. Sadeghpour

- **Description:** Demonstrates 'bump-ramp-plateau' behavior in spectral form factor for driven 1D cold-atom models, studying ETH signatures through quantum chaos diagnostics.

- **Title:** Observation of many-body dynamical localization
- **Authors:** Yanliang Guo et al.
- **Year:** 2025

## 6. Key Themes and Findings

### 6.1 ETH Verification Methods
- **Level statistics:** Wigner-Dyson distribution indicates quantum chaos and ETH compliance
- **Entanglement entropy:** Volume-law scaling in ETH systems vs area-law in MBL
- **Diagonal matrix elements:** Smooth functions of energy in ETH systems
- **Off-diagonal matrix elements:** Gaussian distribution with exponentially small variance

### 6.2 ETH Violation Mechanisms
- **Many-body localization (MBL):** Disorder-induced localization preventing thermalization
- **Quantum scars:** Special eigenstates with low entanglement entropy
- **Hilbert space fragmentation:** Symmetry-induced breaking into disconnected sectors
- **Weak ergodicity breaking:** Majority of eigenstates obey ETH but polynomial number violate

### 6.3 Computational Method Limitations
- **ED:** Limited to small system sizes (L~20-24 for excited states)
- **Tensor networks:** Limited by entanglement barrier for thermalizing systems
- **Cold atom experiments:** Limited by decoherence and measurement timescales

## 7. Recommendations for Further Research

1. **Combined approaches:** Use ED for small systems to validate tensor network results for larger systems
2. **Cold atom experiments:** Focus on quasiperiodic disorder for potential stable MBL in 2D
3. **QMC methods:** Develop QMC approaches specifically for ETH studies
4. **Prethermalization:** Explore tunable prethermalization stages for quantum information applications
