## Hypothesis

Route-local fermion actions can generate Hamiltonian and angular-momentum
neighbors without enumerating a basis. If equal target determinants are merged
before amplitude evaluation, sparse local estimators retain the exact fermion
signs while their cost follows occupied source pairs, nonzero target pairs, and
the two composed one-body ladder passes. Because the benchmark Hamiltonian
stores `H[target, source]`, a Hermitian local-energy row must use the conjugate
of each accumulated source-column coefficient for genuinely complex inputs.

single hypothesis was therefore tested in a disposable script: express each

with `ldexp` on its real and imaginary components.

That experiment exactly reproduced both formerly asymmetric finite results,
preserved a complex-phase power-of-two result at scale `2**950`, and raised
