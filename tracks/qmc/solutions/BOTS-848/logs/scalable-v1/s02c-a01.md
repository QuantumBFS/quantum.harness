## Exact-action feasibility evidence

Two independent implementation reviews reached the same blocking result.

1. The verified identity

   ```text
   S_ell = c_ell N I + sum_{i<j} p_ell(J_i dot J_j)
   ```

   reconstructs the projected-density scalar at `2Q=15/21` and
   `ell=2,3,4` to residuals between about `3e-16` and `7e-12`. The obstacle is
   therefore evaluation cost, not the scalar identity.
2. The frozen product contains the seed, three depth-one amplitudes, and nine
   depth-two amplitudes. Exact depth two requires mixed JK-seed derivatives of
   total order as high as eight. A generic contracted-jet envelope contains
   `9,969` mixed derivatives for `N=6` and `34,113` for `N=8`. At the frozen
   batches, the minimum matrix/inverse jet storage was estimated at about
   `2.8 GiB` and `8.5 GiB`, before determinant/inverse algebra and optimizer
   state.
3. Exact pair-Casimir interpolation reproduces the same cost in a different
   representation: at least `983,040` seed evaluations per `N=6`
   configuration and `16,397,920` per `N=8` configuration, estimated at about
   `196 s` and `4713 s` per configuration respectively.
4. A nested-JAX-JVP prototype did not finish even at `N=3`, `ell=2`, depth two
   within `75 s`. Custom VJP changes coefficient differentiation but cannot
   remove the expensive primal operator action.
5. Stochastic pair/operator sampling is not a compliant escape hatch. It can
   make an unbiased complex-amplitude estimate, but `|Psi|^2`, Metropolis
   ratios, and nonlinear VMC gradients then become biased or acquire a
   sign/phase estimator. Replacing the exact scalar, using a multiplicative
   coordinate correlator, switching to JK1/JKk, or using a coefficient-only
   network would violate the frozen Challenge candidate contract.

No Task 4 placeholder model was added because a positive parameter count
without a viable exact pointwise action would be misleading.
