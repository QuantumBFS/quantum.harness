# Paper II cover-letter draft

Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript *Geometric Response of Exactly Degenerate Quantum State Bundles* for publication in [Physical Review B / Physical Review D; select before submission].

The paper asks whether the statistical ideas behind the eigenstate thermalization hypothesis can be formulated for an exactly degenerate protected bundle, where ordinary energy-resolved ETH variables are unavailable. We prove an exact weighted-additivity theorem for complete fourth cumulants of independent centered complex response channels, including covariance and pseudocovariance, and derive an inverse-effective-channel corollary under explicit comparability assumptions. We also give a response-algebra commutant criterion that tests irreducibility without treating it as evidence of Gaussianity or thermalization.

The proposed physical specialization was fixed before validation and tested in a rectangular chiral model with index-protected nullity. The exactness, gap, response, gauge, and covariance checks pass, but all three random-class validation values fall outside the frozen development band. We therefore report the preregistered `random_channel_failure` branch. The positive title *The Geometric ETH* is withheld, and the manuscript explains precisely what the theorem proves, what the numerical test rejects, and what remains unidentified.

The paper also places the result next to source-verified calculations of Berry curvature and Chern data in protected BPS, supersymmetric SYK, and super-JT sectors. It does not claim black-hole thermalization, a universal protected-state ensemble, or an asymptotic Geometric ETH.
