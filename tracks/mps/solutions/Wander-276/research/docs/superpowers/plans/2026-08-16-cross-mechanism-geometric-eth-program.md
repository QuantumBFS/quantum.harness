**Tech Stack:** Python 3, NumPy, SciPy sparse linear algebra, pytest, Matplotlib, Slurm on `shen`/`acamtw70yu` with account `giggleliu`, REVTeX for manuscript integration.

- New task-05 outputs are written below `01_task_folder/task_05/script/output/` and are versioned `v9` or later.
