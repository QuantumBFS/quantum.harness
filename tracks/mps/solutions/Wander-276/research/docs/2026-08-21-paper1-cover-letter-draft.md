# Paper I cover-letter draft

Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript *Exactly Degenerate Quantum Chaos: Non-Abelian Quantum Geometry as a Probe* for publication in Physical Review B.

The paper addresses a basic obstruction in applying conventional quantum-chaos tests to protected many-body systems: an exactly degenerate eigenspace has no internal level spacings, so level statistics and the connected energy spectral form factor are silent. We formulate the alternative problem in terms of the gapped projector bundle over coupling space and calculate its off-fiber response, non-Abelian curvature, connected fourth-order statistics, and Wilson transport.

The manuscript combines exact identities and finite-size calculations across fractional-quantum-Hall parent Hamiltonians, a Moore–Read clustering parent, supersymmetric/cohomological kernels, and stabilizer controls. It separates intrafiber spectral structure from projector motion, shows that local random-matrix-like geometric correlations can coexist with covariance and topological memory, and identifies protection mechanism and accessible tangent algebra as independent sources of structure. The claims are deliberately finite size: the paper does not assert an asymptotic or universal Geometric ETH.

The source package contains a machine-readable evidence registry, seven source-hashed vector figures, complete citation and claim audits, deterministic main and Supplemental Material PDFs, and a fail-closed reproduction command. A related companion manuscript develops a conditional channel theorem and reports a prospectively registered negative test; the two manuscripts do not duplicate numerical sections or prose.
