# Primary-source audit for *The Geometric ETH*

This file is the source registry for Paper II.  A row is included only when the
title, author list, date, and DOI or arXiv identifier can be resolved from the
publisher record, Crossref metadata for the registered DOI, or the official
arXiv record.  The `primary claim` column deliberately states the narrow claim
for which the paper may be cited; it is not a summary of the whole source.
The `verified date` is recorded in the table as `verified_date` so that the
registry can be consumed by both readers and automated checks.

The registry is intended to separate three statements that are often conflated:
(i) ordinary ETH and spectral chaos, (ii) geometry of a degenerate state bundle,
and (iii) BPS, SYK, JT-gravity, and black-hole applications.  A standard ETH
reference does not by itself establish a claim about Berry curvature, and a
BPS-protection reference does not by itself establish chaos.

## Named-author ambiguity and manuscript boundary

## Internal companion record

`wang2026exactdegenerate` denotes the frozen companion Paper I, *Exactly Degenerate Quantum Chaos: Non-Abelian Quantum Geometry as a Probe*.  It is an internal unpublished manuscript rather than one of the 49 external primary-source records above.  Paper II cites it only for the provenance of the condensed-matter evidence registry and does not use it to validate the prospective chiral result.

The official record for the work referred to as “it from ETH” is
[arXiv:2505.20385](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20385), whose author list is Hao
Geng, Ling-Yan Hung, and Yikun Jiang.  The query name **Lingxin Kong** (孔令欣)
does not occur in that primary record, and the search of the official arXiv and
publisher records did not identify a unique quantum-gravity or ETH paper by
Lingxin Kong that could replace Ling-Yan Hung.  The name is therefore recorded
as an unresolved identification ambiguity and is excluded from the Paper II
bibliography and manuscript claims.  No author attribution to Lingxin Kong is
made by this audit.
