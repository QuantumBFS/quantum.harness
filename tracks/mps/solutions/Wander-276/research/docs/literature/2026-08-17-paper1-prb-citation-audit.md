## Claim-to-source conclusion

The cited literature supports the definitions, parent-Hamiltonian mechanisms, control models, and relation to earlier geometric-chaos work. None of these references establishes the new finite-rank fourth-order result reported here; that claim is tied to the repository's registered numerical evidence and frozen inference protocol. Conversely, Paper I does not use the cited literature to claim an asymptotic or universal Geometric ETH.
