\section{Scope of the gravitational comparison}
\label{app:literature-scope}

The source audit contains 49 records with unique DOI or arXiv identifiers.  Its purpose is bibliographic and logical: it states the narrow claim for which each paper is used and does not treat citations as additional numerical observations.  The five statements about D1/D5 half-BPS horizonless states, quarter-BPS $\mathcal N=4$ super-Yang--Mills states, $\mathcal N=2$ super-JT gravity, $\mathcal N=2$ SYK Berry curvature, and large Chern numbers are all traced to the direct calculation of Ref.~\cite{chen2026berry}.  We do not multiply the apparent evidence by citing papers that only supply surrounding SYK, JT, or AdS/CFT background.

The named ``It from ETH'' connection refers to Ref.~\cite{itfrometh2025}, authored by Hao Geng, Ling-Yan Hung, and Yikun Jiang.  The name Lingxin Kong does not occur in that primary record and was not uniquely matched to a relevant source.  It is therefore excluded from the author attribution and from manuscript claims.

None of the Paper-I condensed-matter models enters the effective-channel plot as an archival point.  Their stored registry does not contain response covariances indexed by a documented independent Hamiltonian or disorder unit, so an $N_{\rm eff}$ value would require an imputation or a change of exchangeability unit.  The retrospective comparison is restricted to mechanism and claim boundaries and does not enter the Paper-II title gate.
