\section{Gauge-covariant response}
\label{app:response}

Let \(P\) project onto the exact zero-energy fiber and \(\bar P=1-P\). Differentiating \(HP=0\) and projecting onto \(\bar P\) gives \(\bar P(\partial_aP)P=-\bar PH^+\bar P(\partial_aH)P\), where \(H^+\) is the inverse on the positive-energy complement. With \(X_a=\bar PH^+\bar P(\partial_aH)P\), the sign disappears from every quadratic response. In a local frame of the fiber,
\begin{equation}
\cQ_{ab}=X_a^\dagger X_b,\qquad g_{ab}=\frac{\cQ_{ab}+\cQ_{ba}}{2},\qquad F_{ab}=i(\cQ_{ab}-\cQ_{ba}).
\label{eq:supp-qgt}
\end{equation}
If the fiber frame changes by \(U\), then \(X_a\mapsto X_aU^\dagger\), so \(\cQ_{ab}\mapsto U\cQ_{ab}U^\dagger\). Traced products and eigenvalues are therefore gauge invariant.

The response convention uses the complete positive complement. A numerically small internal bandwidth, by itself, would not suffice: every opened kernel also passes its expected-rank, residual, orthonormality, and positive-gap checks before any fourth-order statistic is evaluated.

\section{Moore--Read coherent-state parent}
\label{app:mr}

For \(N\) bosons in \(N_\phi\) lowest-Landau-level orbitals, define one coherent annihilation operator \(b_x=\sum_j\phi_{xj}b_j\) at each point of an \(N_\phi\times N_\phi\) magnetic-translation orbit. The constraint matrix stacks \(C_x=b_x^3/\sqrt{3!}\) from the \(N\)-particle basis to the \((N-3)\)-particle basis. The parent is stored as \(H=C^\dagger C\), so its zero modes are the right kernel of the sparse factor. The full-column-rank coherent frame resolves the translational three-body constraint while avoiding assembly of a dense many-body Hamiltonian.

The opened fixed-four-quasihole cases have basis dimensions \(\MRNFourBasis\) and \(\MRNSixBasis\), exact zero-mode ranks \(\MRNFourRank\) and \(\MRNSixRank\), and first positive eigenvalues \(\MRNFourGap\) and \(\MRNSixGap\). The observed ranks agree with the cyclic \((2,2)\)-admissible root count associated with the bosonic Moore--Read parent \cite{mooreread1991,zhang2023parent}. Every local panel contains normalized guiding-center-density tangents, and every response vector is checked against the factorized linear equation before inclusion.

The opened panel medians are \(R_4=\MRNFourRFour\) and \(\MRNSixRFour\). These are descriptive within-panel residuals. The inferential result instead uses all \PanelPairCount\ unordered pairs of the \PanelCount\ panels, as described in Sec.~\ref{app:statistics}.

\section{Lattice supersymmetry and the Hodge split}
\label{app:lattice-susy}

On a graph \(G\), the constrained creation operator \(d_i^\dagger=c_i^\dagger\prod_{j\sim i}(1-n_j)\) defines \(Q=\sum_i z_i d_i^\dagger\). Fermionic signs and the hard-core constraint give \(Q^2=0\). In fixed charge \(f\),
\begin{equation}
H_f=Q_{f-1}Q_{f-1}^\dagger+Q_f^\dagger Q_f,
\label{eq:hodge}
\end{equation}
so the zero modes represent \(Q\)-cohomology classes \cite{fendley2003lattice,huijse2010cohomology}. The positive complement splits orthogonally into exact and coexact images. Differentiating both terms in Eq.~\eqref{eq:hodge} supplies two response branches; their direct sum is checked against the full pseudoinverse response.

For a disjoint union of \(m=\LatticeCycleSizes\) six-site cycles in charge \(2m\), the fixed-charge basis dimensions are \(\LatticeBasisDims\) and the cohomology ranks are \(\LatticeRanks\). Both exact and coexact branches are nonzero. The registered directional estimates are \(\LatticeCumulants\), with one-sided \(p\)-values \(\LatticePValues\). The corresponding full normalized cumulant norms are \(\LatticeCumulantNorms\). Thus the negative gate concerns one frozen direction and does not imply a vanishing tensor.

Disjoint cycles were chosen because the cohomology rank and Hodge decomposition can be audited exactly while the fiber dimension grows. They do not test spatial mixing on a connected lattice. A connected graph family is required before claiming a local supersymmetric thermodynamic class.

\section{X-cube controls}
\label{app:xcube}

The periodic X-cube stabilizer table has \(3L^3\) edge qubits. Binary symplectic elimination gives stabilizer rank \(3L^3-(6L-3)\), hence ground-space dimension \(2^{6L-3}\) for every opened \(L=\XCubeLengths\). Positive coefficient changes in a fixed commuting stabilizer Hamiltonian preserve every common eigenspace; consequently \(\partial_aP=0\), \(g=0\), and \(F=0\).

For the moving-projector control, choose a vertex-plane stabilizer \(S\) containing edge \(e\) and Hermitian generators \(G=X_e/2\), \(K=iX_eS/2\). Both Pauli operators are detectable, their product differs from \(S\) by a phase, and they anticommute. With \(P(\lambda,\mu)=e^{-i\lambda G}e^{-i\mu K}P e^{i\mu K}e^{i\lambda G}\), direct Pauli algebra gives
\begin{equation}
iP[\partial_\lambda P,\partial_\mu P]P=-\frac{1}{2}P.
\label{eq:supp-xcube}
\end{equation}
The curvature therefore has one distinct eigenvalue with multiplicity \(2^{6L-3}\) and connected variance \(\XCubeConnectedVariance\). A two-qubit dense realization verifies both Eq.~\eqref{eq:supp-xcube} and exact isospectrality to machine precision; the production sizes use only the binary-symplectic proof and never allocate the exponentially large ground space.

\section{Complete covariance and conditional inference}
\label{app:statistics}

Let \(r=1,\ldots,R\) label tangent panels at one fixed parent Hamiltonian, with \(R=\PanelCount\). After whitening the tangent-label covariance, define \(T^{(r)}_{abcd}=D^{-1}\Tr(X_a^{(r)\dagger}X_b^{(r)}X_c^{(r)\dagger}X_d^{(r)})\). For each distinct ordered pair \(r\ne s\), the code evaluates all three complex Wick contractions in a common ambient Hilbert space. Their average is subtracted from \(R^{-1}\sum_rT^{(r)}\) to obtain \(\widehat\cK\). The implementation uses every one of the \(R(R-1)/2=\PanelPairCount\) unordered pairs and explicitly symmetrizes the two orders.

The registered scalar is the real projection of \(\widehat\cK\) onto the unit tensor proportional to \(\delta_{ab}\delta_{cd}+\delta_{ad}\delta_{cb}\), normalized by the Frobenius norm of the complete Wick tensor. Delete-one cumulants produce jackknife pseudovalues and their standard error. The Moore--Read values have \(z=\MRNFourZ\) and \(\MRNSixZ\), with one-sided \(p=\MRNFourP\) and \(\MRNSixP\). An independently generated augmented-covariance Gaussian oracle agrees with the factorized three-pairing implementation to relative error below the registered numerical tolerance.

Panels are deterministically seeded samples from a local tangent construction while the Hamiltonian and protected projector remain fixed. The reported \(p\)-values are therefore conditional on treating those panels as exchangeable. They quantify reproducibility over that panel family; they do not estimate disorder-to-disorder or Hamiltonian-to-Hamiltonian uncertainty. No asymptotic statement uses these \(p\)-values.

\section{Claim boundary and reproducibility}
\label{app:claims}

The established statements are: exact ranks and gaps for the opened Moore--Read and lattice-SUSY cases; exact X-cube degeneracy formulas and two structured geometric controls; positive covariance-complete Moore--Read panel cumulants at both opened sizes; and failure of the same positive direction in all three opened lattice-SUSY sizes.
