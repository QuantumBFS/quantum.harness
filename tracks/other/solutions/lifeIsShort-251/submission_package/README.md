## Mathematical status

The proposed paper has one central contribution: every one-vertex incident-edge marginal of a weighted random-forest measure is real stable, including after feasible coordinate conditioning outside the star. The proof uses a component-marked forest polynomial and an exact differential-unrooting identity. The appendix gives a coefficientwise proof audit. The finite regression script is diagnostic only; no theorem depends on computation.

The paper does **not** claim negative correlation for disjoint edges or global strong Rayleighness. The earlier cyclomatic-number result has been removed from the paper because its conclusion is substantially covered by prior small-regular-matroid results and 2-sum closure.
