## Plain-text abstract

Let a random forest of a finite loopless multigraph have probability proportional to the product of its nonnegative edge activities. For every vertex, we prove that the multivariate generating polynomial of the set of incident edges present in the forest is real stable. Thus every incident-edge marginal is strongly Rayleigh. The conclusion remains valid after arbitrary feasible coordinate conditioning on edges outside the star. It follows that incident edges are negatively associated under such conditioning, every pair of adjacent edges is negatively correlated for arbitrary inhomogeneous activities, and the number of selected edges in any part of a star has a Poisson-binomial distribution.

More generally, for a weighted graph K, a component fugacity q >= 0, and vertex variables x, we prove that the component-marked forest polynomial

    sum_{F forest} y^F product_{C component of F} (q + sum_{u in C} x_u)

is real stable in x. It is obtained from the rooted matrix-forest determinant by the differential-unrooting identity

    product_{ij in E(K)} (1 - q y_ij partial_i partial_j)
    det(q I + L_K + diag(x)).

Each factor preserves multiaffine real stability. The full unrooted forest polynomial need not itself be stable, even for a triangle, so the result is local and does not resolve the open negative-correlation problem for disjoint edges.

## HTML abstract for E-JC

Let a random forest of a finite loopless multigraph have probability proportional to the product of its nonnegative edge activities. For every vertex, we prove that the multivariate generating polynomial of the set of incident edges present in the forest is real stable. Thus every incident-edge marginal is strongly Rayleigh. The conclusion remains valid after arbitrary feasible coordinate conditioning on edges outside the star. It follows that incident edges are negatively associated under such conditioning, every pair of adjacent edges is negatively correlated for arbitrary inhomogeneous activities, and the number of selected edges in any part of a star has a Poisson-binomial distribution.

More generally, for a weighted graph \(K\), a component fugacity \(q\geq 0\), and vertex variables \(x\), we prove that

\[
\sum_{F\text{ forest}} y^F\prod_{C\in\operatorname{Comp}(F)}
\left(q+\sum_{u\in C}x_u\right)
\]

is real stable in \(x\). It is obtained from the rooted matrix-forest determinant by the differential-unrooting identity

\[
\prod_{ij\in E(K)}\left(1-qy_{ij}\partial_i\partial_j\right)
\det\!\left(qI+L_K+\operatorname{diag}x\right).
\]

Each factor preserves multiaffine real stability. The full unrooted forest polynomial need not itself be stable, even for a triangle, so the result is local and does not resolve the open negative-correlation problem for disjoint edges.
