## Challenge

| Row | |
|---|---|
| **Challenge** | Can a general neural-network ansatz represent the renormalized Hamiltonian in variational Monte Carlo renormalization group (VMCRG) more expressively than the traditional nearest- / next-nearest-neighbour coupling expansion — cutting the variational bias introduced at each RG iteration on the 2D Ising model at 45×45, and, if it holds, reaching the 3D spin glass at 45³ to locate the transition point. |
| **Catalog issue** | `Addresses #28` — "Use neural network to represent renormalized Hamiltonians for classical spin systems", released by Yantao Wu, Institute of Physics, CAS. |
| **Track** | `tracks/other/` — the issue names no solution folder, and its `Method` field ("Monte Carlo sampling, variational calculation, renormalization group") spans `qmc` and `mps` without sitting in either; classical Monte Carlo + RG + neural networks filed under `other`. Our call. |
| **Reference** | Phys. Rev. Lett. **119**, 220602 (2017) — [arXiv:1707.08683](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.08683), as cited in the challenge issue. |
