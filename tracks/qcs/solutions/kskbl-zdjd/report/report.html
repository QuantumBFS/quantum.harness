⟦zh⟧B 的收益来自先做代数消除，再利用减法器与全加器之间的结构同构。⟦en⟧B improved first through algebraic cancellation, then through the structural equivalence between subtractors and full adders.

53 → 51 → 49⟦zh⟧总计减少 4 门，完整验证 16,384 组输入和 1,500 条训练数据。⟦en⟧Four gates saved, verified on all 16,384 inputs and 1,500 training rows.

⟦zh⟧第一步：消掉两次异或⟦en⟧Step one: cancel two XORs

⟦zh⟧在原始的条件二补码阶段，最低输出位连续执行的两个 XOR 在代数上抵消。精确库映射识别出这一点，53 门直接降到 51 门。⟦en⟧In the original conditional two’s-complement stage, two consecutive XOR operations on the least-significant output cancel algebraically. Exact-library mapping exposed this and reduced 53 gates to 51.

⟦zh⟧第二步：把全减器看成全加器⟦en⟧Step two: reinterpret the full subtractor

⟦zh⟧全减器可写成输入为非 x、y 和借位的全加器；其进位就是下一位借位，而和位的补正好免费得到差位。基于全加器三输入对称性生成的 50 个种子，经八层 SAT 窗口后全部收敛到 49 门。⟦en⟧A full subtractor can be written as a full adder fed by complemented x, y and the borrow. Its carry is the next borrow, while the complemented sum yields the difference bit for free. Fifty symmetry-derived seeds all converged to 49 gates after eight-level SAT-window optimization.

⟦zh⟧结果解释⟦en⟧Interpretation ⟦zh⟧在本任务的计分模型下，把全减器改写为带输入和输出极性的全加器形式，使优化结果由 51 门降到 49 门。该结果说明算术单元的表示方式会影响映射结果，但不说明该改写对其他门库或位宽同样有效。⟦en⟧Under this task’s cost model, rewriting the full subtractor as a full adder with input and output polarity reduced the mapped result from 51 to 49 gates. This shows that arithmetic-cell representation affects mapping in this instance; it does not establish the same benefit for other libraries or bit widths.

⟦zh⟧任务 C：六位乘法⟦en⟧Task C: Six-bit Multiplication

⟦zh⟧C 的原始门数最高，所用搜索流程也最多。常规部分积压缩树在已测试流程中的最低结果为 166 门；更低结果分别来自大型多输出区域综合、公开构造复现和全局可达状态约束。⟦en⟧C has the largest initial gate count and received the broadest set of search procedures. The lowest result obtained from the tested conventional partial-product trees was 166 gates. Lower counts came from large multi-output region synthesis, reproduction of a published construction, and globally reachable-state constraints.

168 → 156⟦zh⟧减少 12 门；当前候选通过全部 4,096 组输入和 1,200 条训练数据。⟦en⟧Twelve gates saved; the current candidate passes all 4,096 inputs and all 1,200 training rows.

⟦zh⟧阶段一：SAT 局部无关项优化，168 到 166⟦en⟧Phase I: SAT-derived local don’t-care optimization, 168 to 166

⟦zh⟧基线由 36 个部分积门、24 个五门全加器和 6 个两门半加器组成，恰好是 168 门。高强度 mfs2 使用 SAT 推导局部无关项，先得到 167，再用更大的六层窗口得到 166。之后生成 100 个随机压缩顺序种子，17 个也到达 166，但没有一个继续下降。⟦en⟧The baseline contains 36 partial-product gates, 24 five-gate full adders and 6 two-gate half adders, exactly 168 gates. High-effort mfs2 with SAT-derived local don’t-cares first reached 167, then 166 with a larger six-level window. One hundred randomized compressor-order seeds were generated; 17 also reached 166, but none went lower.

⟦zh⟧166 门阶段的结构性观察⟦en⟧Structural observations at 166 gates
  • ⟦zh⟧基线中的全加器和半加器分别使用五门和两门；已完成的小型精确综合没有找到更低的独立实现。⟦en⟧Full and half adders use five and two gates, respectively; completed small exact-synthesis tests did not find cheaper independent implementations.
  • ⟦zh⟧独立替换某个输出锥会复制原本与高位共享的中间进位。⟦en⟧Replacing a single output cone duplicates intermediate carries shared with higher bits.
  • ⟦zh⟧局部切口把边界信号视为任意组合，会丢掉乘法器内部的可达性约束。⟦en⟧Local cuts treat boundary signals as arbitrary combinations and lose multiplier reachability constraints.

⟦zh⟧阶段二：166 到 165，大型联合张量区域⟦en⟧Phase II: 166 to 165 through a large joint tensor region

⟦zh⟧小型扇出自由锥均未发现改进，于是我们转而把跨越多条乘法对角线的区域收缩成多输入、多输出布尔张量。一个 22 门、7 输入、5 输出的低位区域经过关系综合得到 21 门实现；128 个边界状态全部一致，嵌回全局后 4,096 组输入仍完全正确，于是 C 到达 165。⟦en⟧Small fanout-free cones yielded no improvement, so the search contracted regions spanning several multiplication diagonals into multi-input, multi-output Boolean tensors. Relation synthesis reduced one 22-gate, seven-input, five-output low-product region to 21 gates. All 128 boundary states matched, and the embedded circuit remained exact on all 4,096 inputs, producing the 165-gate result.

⟦zh⟧阶段三：采用不同乘法器构造，165 到 158⟦en⟧Phase III: replacing the multiplier construction, 165 to 158

⟦zh⟧对 165 门网络进行的后续局部和联合区域搜索没有产生更小结果。随后直接重建 Sergeev 给出的 158 门乘法器，其中包含 36 个部分积、6 个半加器、3 个普通全加器、1 个编码输入全加器、10 个改进双全加器，以及 11 个用于构造编码对的 XOR。165 到 158 对应整体构造替换，不能解释为在 165 门网络中删除了七个局部门。⟦en⟧Subsequent local and joint-region searches on the 165-gate network did not produce a smaller result. We then directly reconstructed Sergeev’s 158-gate multiplier, containing 36 partial products, 6 half adders, 3 ordinary full adders, 1 encoded-input full adder, 10 modified double full adders, and 11 XOR gates used to form encoded pairs. The change from 165 to 158 is a replacement of the complete construction, not the deletion of seven local gates from the 165-gate network.

⟦zh⟧阶段四：158 到 156，只在可达状态上成立⟦en⟧Phase IV: 158 to 156 using reachable states

⟦zh⟧全域语义重代入为每条内部线计算了覆盖全部 12 位输入的真值向量。它发现最高乘积位的四门扇出自由锥可以由两门替换。若把三个边界信号当作独立变量，该替换并不成立；但在真实乘法器的 4,096 个可达输入上，它完全等价。⟦en⟧Full-domain semantic resubstitution computed a truth vector over all twelve primary inputs for every internal wire. It discovered that the four-gate fanout-free cone driving the most significant product bit could be replaced by two gates. The identity is false if the three boundary signals are independent, yet it is exact on all 4,096 states reachable in the real multiplier.

g159 = NAND ~g29 ~g154

⟦zh⟧结论范围⟦en⟧Scope of the conclusion ⟦zh⟧158 是构造性上界，不是六位乘法的最优性证明。158 到 156 的结果表明，在本电路中，全局可达状态约束能够产生普通局部真值表未表示的简化机会。是否还存在其他此类区域尚未确定。⟦en⟧The 158 figure is a constructive upper bound, not an optimality proof for six-bit multiplication. The reduction from 158 to 156 shows that, in this circuit, globally reachable-state constraints can expose a simplification not represented by an ordinary local truth table. It remains unknown whether additional regions of this kind exist.
