The question

What did we actually try to certify?

The target is not a lower bound on the ground-state energy of a finite Hamiltonian. It is a decreasing sequence of upper bounds on a locally non-degenerate spectral gap in the infinite lattice, obtained by excluding hypothesized gaps with state-polynomial SDP infeasibility.

Ordinary moment SDP

Finite ground-energy relaxation

min ℒ(H)   subject to   ℒ(1)=1, Md(ℒ) ⪰ 0

Every physical state is feasible, so the objective gives a lower bound on the modeled ground energy.

not the target method
Issue #92 method

Infinite-volume assumed-gap feasibility

ω(a†[H,a]) ≥ γ [ ω(a†a) − |ω(a)|² ]

At fixed γ, exact infeasibility excludes an infinite-volume state with gap at least γ.

thermodynamic upper-bound direction
The one-way rule: infeasible means “γ is excluded”; feasible means only “not excluded at this finite relaxation.” A feasible point is never reported as a gap lower bound.
Algorithm

From a local commutator to an SDP

For local a, only finitely many Hamiltonian terms fail to commute with it. The finite region is therefore a commutator window with a buffer, not a finite sample whose boundary spectrum is extrapolated.

[H,a] = ∑X : X∩supp(a)≠∅ [hX,a] The sum is finite for every local excitation, even though H is an infinite formal sum.
1 · Windowrooted excitation support plus one interaction shell
2 · Algebraexact finite matrix-unit canonicalization
3 · Liftstate symbols σ(w) and products of expectations
4 · SDPmoment, stationarity, covariance, and gap PSD blocks
5 · Excludefixed-γ infeasibility gives an upper-bound candidate

Why state polynomials are necessary

The variance contains the nonlinear quantity |ω(a)|². Introduce a commuting symbol σ(w) for each operator expectation and let a second linear functional act on products σ(u)σ(v). At fixed γ, the lifted entries are affine.

Finite-level certificate direction

Every genuine gapped infinite-volume state maps to a feasible pseudo-moment point. Omitting higher constraints only enlarges the feasible set. Therefore exact infeasibility remains sound even for a weak outer relaxation.

Numerical caveat: the mathematical implication of exact SDP infeasibility is rigorous. A floating Clarabel status is not yet a machine-checkable proof; the project does not hide that distinction.

What not to do next

Do not spend the next stage on a much larger open-ball ED run. The demonstrated blocker is the conditioning and completeness of the thermodynamic state-polynomial SDP, not a shortage of finite-patch spectra.

Final claim boundary in one paragraph

We have a tested exact cutoff algebra, validated hyperbolic graph construction, the complete matrix-algebra hierarchy engine, and a complete-level atomic check whose γ=0.51 exclusion passed exact projection, 256-bit algebraic sign checks, PSD verification, and a positive normalized Farkas margin. Its ρ0 lower and upper duals also pass separate exact projection and PSD checks. We retain the older weak U(1)-restricted radius-one thermodynamic outer SDP and exact finite-open-patch diagnostics. We do not yet have a geometry-sensitive nested numerical comparison, the unrestricted production comparison, or independently verified non-atomic exclusions. Consequently, the non-atomic gap numbers remain floating candidates or unresolved outcomes, never final certified theorems.
