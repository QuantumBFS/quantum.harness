Prototype methods
-----------------
1. Physics-gated tensor promotion checks transfer fixed-point residuals,
   reduced-density-matrix Hermiticity, trace, positive semidefiniteness, local
   spectral range, and normalization overlap before shallow/deep RG promotion.
2. Staged D10-to-D14 promotion routes reproducible candidates through tensor
   search, fixed-point and physical gates, shallow SDP, deep SDP, and exact
   certificate freezing.
3. Saved-dual direct freeze checks charge-block ordering, reconstructs float
   slacks, performs scalar repair and rationalization, builds the exact witness,
   and then invokes the standard proof gate without resolving the large SDP.

Public-package boundary
-----------------------
The current PR publishes the audited algorithm description and this provenance
record. The research-workspace prototype scripts and their targeted tests are
outside the formal self-contained package. The formal depth-47 certificate is
generated and verified entirely by the public xxzcert implementation included
in the submission.
