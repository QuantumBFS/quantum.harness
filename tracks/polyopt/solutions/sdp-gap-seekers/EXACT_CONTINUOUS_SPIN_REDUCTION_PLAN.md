# Exact continuous-spin reduction plan

## Scope

This is a candidate exact feasible-set reduction after the proved full-spin
isotypic model. It does not change the Hamiltonian, local-consistency window,
state class, polynomial degree, or gamma. No solver model may use it until a
separate exhaustive exact truth gate passes.

The Hamiltonian is invariant under the global spin action of `SO(3)`. Given
any feasible unrestricted functional, Haar averaging over that compact group
preserves normalization, stationarity, every positive block, and every gap
block. Hence feasibility can be tested in the globally spin-invariant
subspace.

## Why the degree-two relaxation is unusually simple

Every scalar `MomentKey` in this relaxation contains at most four Pauli
factors. The preceding conjugation and proper signed-axis reductions retain
only moments with even X, Y, and Z parities, so only tensor ranks 0, 2, and 4
remain.

For the vector representation of `SO(3)`, the invariant tensors at those
ranks are:

```text
rank 0:  1
rank 2:  delta_ab
rank 4:  delta_ab delta_cd,
         delta_ac delta_bd,
         delta_ad delta_bc.
```

Thus each axis-erased rank-two moment skeleton has at most one scalar
coordinate, and each rank-four skeleton has at most three. Commutativity of
state symbols can identify some of the three rank-four pairings; those
identifications must be found by exact row reduction rather than assumed.

## Deterministic exact parameterization

For each axis-erased moment skeleton:

1. enumerate all `3^r` axis assignments, with `r <= 4`;
2. canonicalize the resulting `MomentKey` and map every even-parity component
   to its already-proved full-spin orbit representative;
3. evaluate the one or three delta-pairing tensors on every assignment;
4. impose exact equality of pairing rows whenever canonicalization or the
   existing quotient maps two assignments to the same moment;
5. compute the rational nullspace of those equalities;
6. choose lexicographically first independent existing moments as pivot
   coordinates; and
7. express every source moment as an exact rational affine-linear polynomial
   in the pivots.

Using existing moments as pivots keeps normalization explicit and avoids
inventing numerical variables without a source interpretation.

## Required truth gate

Before an optimizer-free JuMP model is accepted, the implementation must
verify:

- every retained full-spin moment is parameterized exactly once;
- each pivot maps to its own coordinate;
- the substitution is deterministic across two independent builds;
- all omitted odd-parity tensor components evaluate to exact zero;
- every component tensor is invariant under the exact rational rotation

  ```text
  R = [ 3/5  -4/5   0
        4/5   3/5   0
          0     0   1 ];
  ```

- all 6,104 isotypic block coefficients reconstruct after substitution;
- normalization and the complete equality space reconstruct; and
- the resulting named real PSD cone inventory is unchanged unless a
  separately proved `SO(3)` row-space decomposition is added.

The rotation above has infinite order: if its angle were a rational multiple
of pi, its rational cosine would have to be `0`, `±1/2`, or `±1`, whereas it
is `3/5`. The closed subgroup generated by it and the already-proved
octahedral rotation group cannot be finite or preserve one axis, so its
closure is `SO(3)`. Exact invariance under these generators is therefore
continuous-spin invariance.

## Decision rule

First inventory the exact pivot count under Slurm. Build and solve derived
MOFs only if the quotient is substantial enough to be decision-relevant.
Because the earlier 8,803-to-3,250 moment quotient increased factor fill, a
smaller variable count alone is not evidence of a memory improvement.

If the moment gate passes, test one additional cone theorem separately.
The degree-at-most-two positive-row space decomposes under `SO(3)` as

```text
centered: l=0 multiplicity 36, l=1 multiplicity 45, l=2 multiplicity 36
scalar:   l=0 multiplicity 37, l=1 multiplicity 45, l=2 multiplicity 36.
```

The current discrete-isotypic representation carries two 36-side copies per
family for the two octahedral pieces of the same `l=2` irrep. Continuous-spin
invariance predicts that the S3-standard 36-side block and the retained
nontrivial-character 36-side block are exactly congruent up to fixed rational
row normalization. This could reduce the positive cone sides from

```text
[36,36,36,45,37,36,36,45]
```

to

```text
[36,36,45,37,36,45],
```

or 4,772 packed coordinates including the gap scalar. Do not remove either
copy until every substituted upper-triangle coefficient and the row-map rank
have been replayed exactly.

## Composition with the spatial reflection

The anti-diagonal site reflection commutes with every global spin rotation.
If both independent truth gates pass, their invariant restrictions may
therefore be composed without changing the feasible/nonempty decision.
Because the continuous-spin coordinates are selected source moments rather
than abstract tensor parameters, the induced spatial action need not be a
permutation of those pivots. It is nevertheless an exact rational
involution.

Let `q_c(x)` be the exact continuous-spin substitution of an isotypic moment
`x`, and let `r(x)` be the corrected spatial action (site reflection followed
by full-spin orbit re-representation). For every continuous pivot `y`, define

```text
T(y) = q_c(r(y)).
```

The combined route must prove `T^2=I` over the rationals and the intertwining
identity

```text
T(q_c(x)) = q_c(r(x))
```

for every source isotypic moment. The fixed space is then found from the
exact row-reduced system `(I-T)y=0`, with existing free pivot moments kept as
coordinates. Every continuous-spin cone coefficient and equality is
projected through that substitution.

On the row side, reuse the already-proved signed spatial involution on each
isotypic block. Split only the six positive `SO(3)` multiplicity cones and
the scalar gap cone, require full rank of every plus/minus row basis, and
replay all spatial cross entries as exact zeros after the combined moment
projection. The combined model is authorized only if:

- both standalone continuous-spin and corrected spatial gates have passed;
- the rational pivot action is an involution and the full intertwining test
  passes;
- the fixed-coordinate substitution is deterministic across two builds;
- every retained pivot remains used by a cone coefficient or equality;
- every spatial row basis has full source rank and every cross block is
  exactly zero; and
- optimizer-free JuMP construction and MOF reload reproduce all named cone
  dimensions.

This route should be considered only after the six-cone continuous-spin
model is measured. A smaller packed coordinate count is not by itself
evidence of lower Mosek factor fill or peak RSS.
