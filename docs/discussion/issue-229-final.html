Scope correction

This report does not establish issue #229. It validates only character-sector decomposition of an already numerical invariant Hermitian matrix. The original challenge additionally requires construction of a noncommutative word basis and affine moment/localizing matrices, lifting generator symmetries to moment variables, simultaneous reduction of the complete SDP pencil, and equality of dense and reduced SDP optima.

The matrix projector remains usable as one backend step after those missing layers produce the group action on the moment basis. Permutation symmetry and SU(2) also remain deferred.
